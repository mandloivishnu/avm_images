1. Core Concept

Active vs Passive Voice (वाच्य परिवर्तन)

Always V3 (Verb 3rd Form)

In Active Voice, the Subject performs the action. In Passive Voice, the Object is prioritized and receives the action.
गोल्डन नियम: Passive Voice में मुख्य क्रिया का हमेशा 3rd Form (Past Participle - V3) ही प्रयोग होता है!

2. Golden Formulae रामबाण नियम एवं चार्ट
6 Core Rules

Present Simple ➔ is/am/are + V3 MP Board 'Do as Directed'
Active Voice (सक्रिय वाक्य) Subject + Verb + Object
He writes a letter.
Object ➔ New Subject ⇄ by + Agent (Old Subject)
Passive Voice (कर्मवाच्य) Helping Verb + V3
A letter is written by him.
Rule: Obj + is/am/are + V3 + by + Sub
💡
Transformation Trick (पहचान व परिवर्तन):
Present Simple (V1/s/es) को passive में बदलते समय 'is/am/are' के साथ क्रिया का 3rd form (written) लगाया जाता है।
