🚀 Physics Cheat Sheet

Class 12 Visual & Animated Revision - Detailed Version

⚡ Unit 1: Electrostatics

Electric Charges & Fields

Coulomb's Law: Computes the electrostatic force between two point charges: F = k(q₁q₂)/r².
Gauss's Law: Total flux Φ = q_enclosed / ε₀. Crucial for finding the E-field of symmetric charge distributions like wires and sheets.

Potential & Capacitance

Potential (V): Work done per unit charge. Equipotential surfaces intersect E-field lines at 90°.
Capacitors: Store electrical energy (U = ½CV²). Adding a dielectric medium increases capacitance by a factor of K: C = Kε₀A/d.

🔋 Unit 2: Current Electricity

Drift Velocity & Ohm's Law

Drift Velocity: Average velocity of free electrons moving in a conductor under an electric field (I = nAev_d).
Ohm's Law: Current is proportional to voltage (V = IR). Resistance depends on temperature and material.

Kirchhoff's Laws & Circuits

Junction Rule (KCL): Sum of currents entering = sum of currents leaving (conservation of charge).
Loop Rule (KVL): Sum of voltage drops and EMFs in a closed loop is zero (conservation of energy). Used in solving complex circuits and Wheatstone bridge.

🧲 Unit 3: Magnetic Effects

Magnetic Fields (Biot-Savart & Ampere)

Biot-Savart Law: Gives magnetic field from a tiny current element.
Ampere's Law: ∮ B · dl = μ₀I. Used to easily find the magnetic field inside a long solenoid or straight wire.

Moving Coil Galvanometer & Magnetism

Detects small currents based on the principle that a current-carrying coil experiences torque (τ = NIAB sinθ) in a magnetic field.
Magnetism: Bar magnets have a dipole moment. Earth acts as a giant magnet with specific elements (declination, dip, horizontal component).

🔄 Unit 4: EMI & AC

Electromagnetic Induction

Faraday's Law: Changing magnetic flux induces an EMF: e = -dΦ/dt.
Lenz's Law: The induced EMF opposes the change that produced it (Conservation of Energy).

Alternating Current & Transformers

LCR Circuits: Resonance occurs when inductive reactance equals capacitive reactance (X_L = X_C), maximizing current.
Transformers: Step up or down AC voltage using mutual induction. Ratio: V_s/V_p = N_s/N_p.

📡 Unit 5: Electromagnetic Waves

EM Spectrum & Properties

Transverse waves of oscillating electric and magnetic fields traveling at light speed (c = 1/√(μ₀ε₀)).
Spectrum Order (Increasing Freq): Radio → Microwave → Infrared → Visible → UV → X-Rays → Gamma Rays.

🌈 Unit 6: Optics

Ray Optics (Lenses & TIR)

Total Internal Reflection (TIR): Occurs when light travels from denser to rarer medium and incidence angle > critical angle (Used in optical fibers).
Lens Maker's Formula: Connects focal length, refractive index, and radii of curvature.

Wave Optics

Huygens Principle: Every point on a wavefront acts as a secondary source.
Interference (Young's Double Slit): Superposition of light causing bright/dark fringes. Fringe width β = λD/d.
Diffraction: Bending of light around sharp corners.

☀️ Unit 7: Dual Nature of Radiation

Photoelectric Effect & Matter Waves

Einstein's Equation: K_max = hν - Φ. Photons knock electrons out of a metal if frequency is above threshold.
De Broglie Wavelength: Moving particles have wave nature: λ = h/p = h/mv.

⚛️ Unit 8: Atoms & Nuclei

Bohr Model & Atomic Spectra

Electrons orbit in specific stationary states where angular momentum is quantized (mvr = nh/2π). Energy is emitted/absorbed only when electrons jump between these orbits.

Nuclei & Binding Energy

Mass Defect: The nucleus mass is slightly less than the sum of its protons/neutrons. This missing mass converts to Binding Energy (E = Δmc²), which holds the nucleus together. High binding energy per nucleon = high stability.

💻 Unit 9: Electronic Devices

Semiconductors & p-n Junctions

p-n Junction: Joining p-type and n-type semiconductors creates a depletion region.
Forward Bias: Conducts current. Reverse Bias: Blocks current.
Rectifiers: Use diodes to convert AC to DC (Half-wave uses 1 diode, Full-wave uses 2).
